How withdrawing works

  • Flexible: request an unstake anytime. After a 2-day cooldown, withdraw. Tokens don't earn during the cooldown, and you can cancel it.
  • Locked (7, 14 or 30 days): can't be withdrawn before the unlock date. When it unlocks, request the withdrawal; after a 2-day cooldown, withdraw.
  • Not withdrawn after unlock? Your tokens stay staked and keep earning at 1×.
  • Rewards can be claimed anytime, locked or not.
