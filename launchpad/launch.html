The rules of the house

  • 🛡️ One template for every token — fixed 1B supply, no mint, no blacklist, no taxes, no owner. Verified once on Blockscout; every launch inherits it. Our scanner marks them safe by construction.
  • 🌊 Born inside a REAL pool — no bonding-curve limbo, no graduation, no migration, ever. Your token is on DexScreener and tradeable by any bot or aggregator from block one. The pool it's born in is the pool it lives in, forever.
  • 🔒 The liquidity is locked for life from second zero. The entire supply sits in a position owned by an ownerless vault with no function to withdraw, move or unlock it. Not the creator, not us, not anyone.
  • 💸 Creators keep 90% of the 1% pool fee on every trade, forever — in ETH, claimable any time (or auto-paid by our bot). Born on Uniswap V3 instead and it's 80%. Hardcoded, immutable, no "we adjusted the split" ever.
  • 🎯 Dev's first buy is hard-capped at 5% of supply — atomic inside the create transaction, so nobody can front-run the creator, and the creator can't corner their own launch at the floor price.
  • 🔥 The protocol's cut is split in stone: half funds the team (fixed address), half flows into the ownerless Burner that market-buys $NLYRA and sends it straight to 0xdEaD. Anyone can pull the trigger — see the Burn Engine above.
  • 🌙 Every launch is announced and read by LYRA — the only launchpad on any chain with a resident AI keeping receipts.
