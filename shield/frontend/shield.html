How it actually works

  1. You deposit and the pool stores a hash (a "commitment") in a Merkle tree. The chain sees a deposit — that part is public, and it's fine.
  2. You keep a secret note. It never leaves your device.
  3. Later, from anywhere, your browser builds a zero-knowledge proof that says: "I know the note of one valid deposit in this tree, and it hasn't been spent" — without revealing which one.
  4. A relayer publishes it and pays the gas. It cannot steal: the recipient is sealed inside the proof. Change one character and the contract rejects it.
  5. Honest by design: an association set — built from our own scanner, public and auditable — keeps proven criminal addresses out. Privacy for honest people, not a laundromat. And if the set ever excludes you unfairly, ragequit returns your funds to your original wallet. No custody, ever.
